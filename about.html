About TipToe Soles

Everyday footwear, made for real comfort.

Our story

Australian owned and operated.

TipToe Soles provides comfortable footwear at affordable prices to customers within Australia and around the world.

Our range is selected for people who want dependable support throughout the day, whether they are at work, walking around town or enjoying a relaxed weekend.

Our team is based at 322 Avondale Road, Enfield NSW 2133, where friendly and practical customer service is at the centre of what we do.
