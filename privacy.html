Information we collect

TipToe Soles may collect information that you choose to provide, including your name, email address, phone number, delivery details and messages sent through contact or feedback forms.

How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • respond to product, order or service enquiries;
  • process and deliver purchases when online ordering is available;
  • manage returns and exchanges;
  • improve the website, footwear range and customer service; and
  • meet legal and record-keeping obligations.

Storage and protection

Reasonable safeguards will be used to protect personal information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. Information will only be kept for as long as it is needed for the stated purpose or required by law.

Sharing information

Personal information will not be sold. It may be provided to trusted service providers where necessary to complete a requested service, such as delivery, payment processing or website support.

Your choices

You may contact TipToe Soles to ask about, correct or update personal information held about you.
