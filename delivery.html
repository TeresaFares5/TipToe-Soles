Dispatching orders

Orders placed before noon AEST are packed and dispatched on business days. Once an order is dispatched with Australia Post, the following delivery estimates apply.

Express shipping

  • Victoria: 1–2 business days
  • NSW, ACT, SA and Queensland metropolitan and most regional areas: 1–3 business days
  • Western Australia, Northern Territory and Tasmania: 2–5 business days

Standard shipping

  • Victoria: 1–3 business days
  • NSW, ACT, SA and Queensland metropolitan and most regional areas: 2–6 business days
  • Western Australia, Northern Territory and Tasmania: 3–10 business days

Outer-regional areas may experience slightly longer delivery times. Public holidays and high-volume periods can also affect estimates.
